# REVIVE — Hackathon Submission

## Track
**TRACK 03 — AI Revenue Recovery**

## Product Name
**REVIVE** — Revenue Intelligence & Verification Engine

## Tagline
> "Recover revenue, not just payments."

## One-Line Description
REVIVE is an autonomous revenue recovery control plane that detects revenue at risk, investigates root causes with AI, simulates recovery strategies, executes bounded interventions through policy gates, and proves actual money recovered through closed-loop measurement.

## Key Innovation
**Counterfactual Recovery Simulator** — For every revenue case, REVIVE simulates multiple intervention strategies and explains why one was chosen, with actual recovery measurement proving impact.

## Technical Highlights
1. **Closed-Loop Recovery** — Prove actual recovery, not just predictions
2. **Policy-Gated AI** — LLM recommends, deterministic systems decide
3. **Experimental Evaluation** — 50k+ transactions, baseline vs REVIVE comparison
4. **Complete Audit Trail** — Every decision traceable from event to outcome
5. **Deterministic Fallback** — System works even without LLM

## Stack
